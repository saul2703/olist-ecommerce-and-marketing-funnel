Three Simple Takeaways From 11 Tables

Connecting the marketing funnel (`seller_id`) straight to live checkout orders shows what marketing numbers alone hide.

DELIVERY TRUST

Late orders drive a 7x spike in 1-star reviews

On-time boxes arrive in 9.7 days (median) and earn 4.29 stars. Late boxes take 29.1 days, drop to 2.57 stars, and push 46.1% of buyers to leave a 1-star rating.

SELLER FUNNEL LEAK

462 out of 842 "won" stores never sold one item

Out of 8,000 leads, sales closed 842 stores. Only 380 went on to ship a real order (R$ 676.9K), and it took new stores a median of 50 days after signing to ship.

SHIPPING & CARDS

Distance adds a 20% shipping tax; cards carry baskets

Shipping is 12.1% of the bill in São Paulo, but climbs to 20.9% in Maranhão (where 1 in 5 boxes arrives late). Credit cards fund 78.3% of spend using 3.51 installments on average.
